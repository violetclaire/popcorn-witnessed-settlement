767-2676.com: witnessed settlement between agents

TESTNET DEMONSTRATION. This page runs the settlement contract's rules in your browser. No real funds move, nothing is deployed from this page, and no POPCORN stamp was purchased or requested for this demo. The Rust contract in this repository (contract/) implements the same rules on NEAR testnet.

Two agents agree terms. The terms are fingerprinted, the fingerprint is bound to the payment, and the money moves only when an independent witness's receipt matches that binding inside the approval window. If the receipt does not match, the money does not move.

Agent A, the payer.
Locks the funds and presents the witness receipt.
Agent B, the payee.
Receives the funds only on a matching receipt.
The witness (POPCORN).
Stamps a fingerprint as received in a witnessed interval, under published keys. Evidence, never permission.

Step 2. Open the settlement

Opening records the payload_digest and the payment identifier, computes the pair_digest that binds them, locks the funds, and fixes the approval window. In this demo the approval window is a one second window at an agreed moment, because a POPCORN receipt's witnessed interval is that moment plus and minus the node's published clock accuracy radius of 10 seconds, and the interval must cover the window.

pair_digest byte form: SHA-256 over the payment identifier bytes, one ":" byte, then the payload_digest bytes, written in base64url. That is the published construction SHA-256(payment_identifier : payload_digest): the two values as text joined by a single colon, no spaces. The form is settled, verified against the worked example in issue #37 of the POPCORN repository.

What a receipt proves, and what it does not

A POPCORN receipt witnesses only that the node received a fingerprint within the stated interval, under a published key. It does not prove agreement timing, authorship, competence, completion, identity, permission, delivery, or execution. Verification depends on accepting the node's key and clock. A receipt is evidence, never permission. The witness never books anyone, never handles the parties' payments beyond the witnessed stamp, and holds no keys from anyone. Agents bring their own keys and check the receipt themselves.
